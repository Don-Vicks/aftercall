Privacy and your data

What stays on this Mac, what leaves it, and what each place keeps.

Where your data lives on this Mac

On this Mac, encrypted ~/Library/Application Support/dev.aftercall.app Audio is deleted after transcription ENCRYPTED
In your macOS Keychain4 items
  • NEAR AI key makes your notes
  • This Mac's NEAR key pays and records proofs, within your caps
  • Data key seals your meetings on disk
  • Backup key opens backups, made from your phrase
Why?

Audio is sealed about once a second while it records, and deleted with its chunk transcripts once your notes are saved. Notes, transcripts and projects are sealed with the data key. The four Keychain items sit under dev.aftercall.app, and the NEAR AI key never reaches the app's window. This keeps other apps, and backup or sync tools, from reading your meetings. It doesn't protect against someone using your Mac while you're signed in, or software running as you that can open your Keychain.

What leaves your Mac 5 places

Where it goes What it sees What it keeps
NEAR AISecure hardware (TEE) Audio in chunks, then the transcript, with project and payee names for spelling. Set by NEAR AI's policy, not by Aftercall.
NEAR blockchainPublic, permanent Proofs: start time, a hash, the model. Payments: payee, amount, a fixed memo. All of it, forever and in public. Never what was said.
Aftercall serverOnly if you use it Recaps you send through Aftercall, for one request. Claim links you make. A send count. Per claim link: token hash, amount, expiry, status, payee account.
ResendRecap email Recaps you send through Aftercall. Sent mail, under Resend's own policy.
1ClickSolana and Base payees Amount, destination address and your NEAR account for refunds. Set by 1Click's policy.
Why?

Draft in Mail, the default, opens a recap in your own mail app, so Aftercall's server never sees it. A claim link keeps the payee's name and the meeting title in the link itself, which browsers never send to a server. NEAR AI's hardware check proves the models ran in a TEE; it doesn't prove what NEAR AI's gateway logs. NEAR RPC also sees the account reads and transactions the app sends.

Delete no undo

Removes every meeting, note, transcript and project here, and Aftercall's four Keychain items. Proofs on NEAR can't be deleted. They hold a hash and the model name, never what was said.
Why?

Payments on NEAR stay too. This Mac's NEAR key stays authorised on your account until you remove it in your wallet, and your agent wallet keeps its balance on NEAR; withdraw it first if you're leaving.
