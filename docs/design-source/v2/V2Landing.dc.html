Private by construction

Where a meeting goes, and what each stop can see. How it works

  1. Your Mac Records both sides, seals the audio, keeps the notes encrypted.
  2. checks first
  3. Gateway TEE Relays the audio over TLS, and the transcript only as ciphertext.
  4. checks first
  5. Whisper and Qwen TEEs Transcribe, then write the notes. The reply comes back signed.
  6. hash only
  7. NEAR Your Mac records a hash of the proof. Never what was said.
Checked before it gets anything
On a typical meeting, 24 checks on the gateway, Whisper and Qwen against a fresh nonce, then 5 on the signed reply. One failure, and nothing is sent there.
End-to-end encrypted summaries
The transcript is sealed on your Mac to the Qwen TEE's attested key. Audio isn't: NEAR AI doesn't offer that for transcription, and the app says so.
Encrypted on your Mac
Audio is sealed as it records and deleted after transcription. Notes are sealed with a key in your Keychain.
Our servers keep nothing
Nothing about a meeting is kept after a response returns. A recap you draft in Mail never reaches them.

What we can't prove

  • That the transcript is accurate: Whisper can mishear names and numbers.
  • That the attested Whisper TEE made it: NEAR AI signs chat replies, not transcriptions.
  • That everyone agreed to be recorded: you confirm it; Aftercall can't check.
  • Who said what: “You” is your mic, and “Others” is the call's audio.

Money that can't run away

Payments come from an agent wallet, not your own. A NEAR contract caps each day and each payment, and pays only people on your list. Over the caps, it waits for your own wallet, so a hijacked app or a tricked AI can't overspend.

  • The app's key can only pay and record proofs
  • Every rule change needs your own wallet

Questions

Does a bot join my call?

No. Aftercall records on your Mac, from your mic and your Mac's audio. You tell people it's recorded, and the app asks you to confirm.

Who can read my notes?

You. Notes stay on your Mac, sealed with a key in your Keychain. What NEAR AI keeps while making them is set by its own policy.

Which call apps work?

Any app on your Mac, including calls in a browser. Aftercall records what your Mac plays and what your mic hears.

What does it cost?

Notes use NEAR AI on your own key, about [cost] per hour of calls. Aftercall is [price]. Payments use the USDC in your agent wallet.

What if a check fails?

Nothing goes to hardware that failed. If the gateway or Whisper fails a check, your audio stays on your Mac.

Can I delete everything?

Yes, on your Mac: deleting a meeting removes its notes and audio. A proof on NEAR stays, but holds only a hash.

Always

Your words reach only secure hardware that has just proven itself, or they never leave your Mac. Our servers keep nothing about a meeting. Nothing said goes on chain.
